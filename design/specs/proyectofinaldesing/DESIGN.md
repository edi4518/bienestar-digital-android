## Brand & Style

This design system targets digital-native teens aged 13–16 navigating algorithmic rabbit holes, endless doomscrolling, and digital dopamine loops. The personality is self-aware, unapologetic, irreverent, and empowering—avoiding clinical or preachy health-tech tropes. The identity synthesizes high-energy Gen-Z digital culture (Discord, BeReal) with the dopamine-driven feedback loops of modern gamification (Duolingo) and the raw technical authority of Neo-Brutalism.

Visual execution centers on:
- **Neo-Brutalist Foundations**: Solid structural weight, high-contrast crisp perimeter strokes, hyper-saturated electric accents, and stark structural boundaries.
- **Gamified Tactility**: Dimensional interactive controls, crisp drop-shadow offsets, sticker-like stat badges, and chunky interactable states that feel physically punchy under touch.
- **Deep Matte Canvas**: Dark graphite backgrounds eliminate light-pollution fatigue while making neon chromatic hits pop with maximum dynamic range.

## Colors

The palette balances deep, low-luminance substrates with high-chroma electric signals:

### Base Canvas & Surfaces
- **App Canvas (`#0E0E12`)**: Deep matte graphite base layer. Non-reflective, zero-glare backdrop.
- **Elevated Card Surface (`#1B1B24`)**: Elevated dark charcoal for modular bento tiles and interactive containers.
- **Layered Surface Deep (`#14141B`)**: Recessed/sunken wells (e.g., progress tracks, inactive containers).
- **Surface Highlight (`#262634`)**: Hover/press active overlay state.

### Boundaries & Structures
- **Structural Stroke (`#2E2E3E`)**: Default 1.5px to 2px high-definition border providing razor-sharp containment on all cards and modules.
- **Subtle Stroke (`#20202C`)**: Internal sub-dividers and secondary separation lines.

### Chromatic Signal & Accents
- **Electric Lime (`#CCFF00`)**: Primary CTA and core action token. Must strictly pair with `#0E0E12` deep black text for AAA contrast.
- **Neon Cyan (`#00F5D4`)**: Secondary accent for algorithms deciphered, network speed, and discovery tasks.
- **Digital Violet (`#9D4EDD`)**: Tertiary accent for mystery quests, algorithmic XP, and level rank markers.
- **Electric Coral (`#FF5733`)**: High-urgency alert, doomscroll breaks, streak warnings, and active countdown timers.
- **Golden Star (`#FFD166`)**: Achievement streaks, multipliers, badge unlocks, and prestige markers.

### Text & Iconography
- **Text Primary (`#F4F4F8`)**: Stark contrast white-tinted slate for titles, headers, and essential stats.
- **Text Secondary (`#9D9DAE`)**: Muted slate for subtext, secondary stats, and meta descriptions.
- **Text On Lime (`#0E0E12`)**: Deep matte black dedicated exclusively to `#CCFF00` surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a dynamic pairing of **Space Grotesk** and **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Space Grotesk** is assigned to all display headlines, numerical stat readouts, streak counters, tags, and gamified badges. Its techno-monospaced roots and quirky geometric terminals deliver brutalist authority without sacrificing high legibility.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** is leveraged across narrative bodies, conversational dialogs, explanatory insights, and long-form tooltips. Its open apertures, warm roundness, and sturdy geometric build balance the tech grit with approachability and swift readability.

Headlines larger than 32px automatically compress via mobile-specific downscaling to preserve rigid bento grid container bounds without aggressive word wraps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an asymmetric, tactile **Bento-box grid** designed specifically for touch-first thumb navigation:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 600px)**: 4-column base structure with a 16px (`1rem`) outer edge padding and uniform 12px or 16px gutters. Modules span full width (4 columns) or split-half (2 columns each) for quick dashboard parity.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (600px - 900px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins, allowing modules to take 2, 4, 6, or 8 columns dynamically.
- **Floating Element Clearance**: Bottom scroll zones are padded by an explicit `88px` minimum bottom margin to ensure full content clearance above the floating pill navigation bar.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred, realistic, diffuse dropshadows. It utilizes **Tactile Neo-Brutalist Hard Offsets** and high-definition surface tiers:

1. **Surface Tiering**:
   - Level 0 (Base): `#0E0E12` canvas.
   - Level 1 (Bento Units): `#1B1B24` with a crisp `1.5px solid #2E2E3E` border.
   - Level 2 (Inner Modules / Floating Badges): `#262634` or solid accent fills.

2. **Hard-Edge Depth**:
   - Interactive cards, buttons, and badges utilize an unblurred solid drop-shadow offset: `0px 4px 0px #000000` or tinted to structural borders (`0px 4px 0px #2E2E3E`).
   - Active press state shifts the element `translate(0, 4px)` while zeroing out the offset shadow (`0px 0px 0px`), creating a satisfying tactile button-press mechanism.

3. **Glow & Aura (Selective)**:
   - Reserved strictly for high-tier gamification moments (e.g., leveling up, high-streak threshold). Neon drop-glows (`0px 0px 20px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.35)`) may only be deployed behind badges and streak flames.

## Shapes

The design language balances fluid pill contours with structural rectangular containers:

- **Bento Card Containers**: Fixed 20px (`1.25rem`) border radius on all four corners. Provides a friendly, curved tactile housing that keeps aggressive neo-brutalist borders playful.
- **Buttons, Badges, and Stat Chips**: 9999px (full pill form factor). High contrast in form against the rectangular cards.
- **Progress Wells**: Sunk capsules with full pill-rounding on outer tracks and inner status meters.

## Components

### 1. Bento Grid Cards
- **Container**: Fill `#1B1B24`, border `1.5px solid #2E2E3E`, corner radius `20px`.
- **Spacing**: 16px internal padding.
- **Variants**: Standard dark tile, accent-tinted hero tile (subtle 5% accent gradient wash overlay), and urgent alert tile (border switched to `1.5px solid #FF5733`).

### 2. Action Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Background `#CCFF00`, text `#0E0E12` (`Space Grotesk`, bold), radius `9999px`. Offset shadow `0px 4px 0px #000000`. Active state: `translateY(4px)` with shadow flattened.
- **Secondary Pill**: Background `#1B1B24`, border `1.5px solid #2E2E3E`, text `#F4F4F8`. Offset shadow `0px 3px 0px #0E0E12`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent fill, text `#9D9DAE`, hover/press fill `#262634`.

### 3. Status Chips & Stat Pills
- **Streak Flame**: Deep charcoal pill with `#FF5733` border, containing fire icon, bold numerical count, and label in `label-md`.
- **XP / Level Chip**: Border `#9D4EDD`, pill fill `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.15)`, text `#F4F4F8`.
- **Algorithm Awareness Tag**: Pill shape with `#00F5D4` dot indicator and `label-sm` monospaced Space Grotesk tracking.

### 4. Progress Bars (Dopamine Meters)
- **Track**: Height 12px or 16px, background `#14141B`, border `1px solid #2E2E3E`, radius `9999px`.
- **Fill**: Solid Electric Lime (`#CCFF00`) or segmented gradient chunks (`#00F5D4` to `#CCFF00`).
- **Segmented Milestone Indicators**: Small vertical 2px cut-outs slicing the bar at 25%, 50%, and 75% intervals.

### 5. Floating Dock Navigation
- **Housing**: Pill-shaped bar floating 20px above the screen bottom edge. Fill `#1B1B24` at 95% opacity with 12px blur backdrop-filter, enclosed in a crisp `1.5px solid #2E2E3E` outline.
- **Icons & Targets**: 4 to 5 touch anchors (Dashboard, Algorithm Feed, Quests, Streaks, Profile). Active tab highlights with an Electric Lime icon and a miniature neon dot underneath.

### 6. Interactive Checkboxes & Toggle Radios
- **Checkboxes**: 22x22px square with rounded-md (6px), 2px solid `#2E2E3E`. When checked: fill `#CCFF00`, stroke `#0E0E12`, checkmark glyph in deep black `#0E0E12`.
- **Toggles**: Chunky pill switch with `#14141B` container and `#CCFF00` round thumb that physically slides with an offset hard shadow.

### 7. Input Fields
- **Container**: Height 52px, fill `#14141B`, border `1.5px solid #2E2E3E`, radius `14px`, text `#F4F4F8`, placeholder `#9D9DAE`.
- **Focus State**: Border shifts to `2px solid #CCFF00` with an immediate zero-blur accent halo or hard black offset.
